## Internship curriculum — six-week journey

- Week 1: Drone foundations & airspace — drone types and components, lift/thrust/stability, intro to DGCA rules and Digital Sky, mission planning and waypoints, live quiz.
- Week 2: Aerodynamics & virtual drone design.
- Week 3: Electronics & drone hardware.
- Week 4: Flight control & programming (ESP32 via Arduino IDE, sensors, PID tuning in a simulator).
- Week 5: Physical kit assembly & first flight.
- Week 6: Flight improvement & final project presentation.
The program includes weekly activities, quizzes, practical submissions and a final project presentation.

## Safety and rules

Drone use must follow the law. In India, check current DGCA requirements and the Digital Sky platform before flying. Basic safety: inspect frame, propellers, motors, wiring and battery before every flight; keep people, animals and property clear; check weather and failsafe settings; never use a swollen or damaged LiPo battery, never leave charging unattended, and disconnect power before changing wiring. Course material is educational, not legal advice. Safety questions: contact@aerocadmy.com.
